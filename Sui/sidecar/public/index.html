Three Distinct Economic Layers Architecture
1. Gameplay Points (Off-Chain): Headshots (+25), Kills (+50), Assists (+15), and Objectives (+100) are recorded off-chain by the trusted game server. No individual Sui transactions are minted during combat.
2. Match Stakes (Isolated Test Mode): Staking commitments remain isolated from scoring and rewards pending formal governance approval.
3. RRC Blockchain Rewards (Batched Settlement): Post-match, the server submits a single batched transaction (settlement::settle_match_batch) distributing approved RRC base units to players' wallets.
