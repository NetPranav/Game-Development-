## What the user wants (their words, condensed)
"Give the enemy a special attack. As soon as it starts, a **proper cinematic scene plays from the side view**: the camera moves to the side, our robot's body **sparks a little** (tiny details), the robot is **blocking the opponent's sword**, the opponent **kicks** our robot, our robot **slides back stabbing its sword into the ground to stop**; then the camera shifts back to the main character. This sequence **always plays when the villain performs this special attack**. Later I will add a few special attacks for the hero too, using animations I download and drop in — so the system must be data-driven: animations are assigned in a data asset and can be swapped without code."

## Deliverable A — `URRCinematicSequence` (data asset, new `RRCinematicSequence.h/.cpp`, `UCLASS(BlueprintType)`, `UPrimaryDataAsset`-free: plain `UDataAsset`)
All with C++ defaults that reproduce the robot-blocks-kick scene using **existing assets** (soft refs, graceful when missing), and every field `EditAnywhere, BlueprintReadWrite`:
- `FRRCineAnim { TSoftObjectPtr<UAnimMontage> Montage; TSoftObjectPtr<UAnimSequence> Sequence; float PlayRate=1; float StartDelay=0; float BlendIn=0.12; float BlendOut=0.2; FName Slot="DefaultSlot"; bool bHoldLastFrame=false; }` — a Montage is used if set, else the Sequence is played as a dynamic montage in `Slot`. Helper `float PlayOn(AActor*, UAnimInstance*)`-style function in the director (see below).
- Roles: `Attacker`, `Defender`. `FRRCineAnim AttackerAnim` (default none: the boss already plays its own attack; this slot lets a *hero* special use the director to play the attacker move), `DefenderBlockAnim` (default `/Game/RR/Player/AM_P_Block.AM_P_Block`, the defender braces **before** contact), `DefenderImpactAnim` (default `/Game/RR/Player/AM_P_HitReact_Front.AM_P_HitReact_Front`, played at contact), `DefenderSlideAnim` (default `/Game/RR/Player/AM_P_Stun.AM_P_Stun`, hold-last-frame, plays during the slide; this is where a "knee + sword stabbed in ground" animation will be dropped in later), `AttackerFollowThroughAnim` (none).
- Timing (seconds from sequence start): `LeadInSeconds` (time from sequence start to contact; **default taken from the boss event's `SecondsToContact`**, otherwise 0.8), `SlideDuration=0.75`, `SlideDistance=420` (cm), `SlideEase` (enum EaseOut/Linear), `HoldAfterSlide=0.55`, `TotalOutBlend=0.5`.
- Camera: `bUseCinematicCamera=true`; shot list `TArray<FRRCineShot> Shots` with `{ float StartTime; FVector LocalOffset (x = along attacker→defender axis, y = SIDE (right side of the axis), z=up), FRotator LookAtOffset; float LookAtHeight; float FOV; float BlendIn (cut=0); float DriftCm (slow dolly along the shot's own direction over the shot's life); float Roll }` with a 3-shot default: (1) wide side shot at contact−0.7 s: side 520, up 130, FOV 50, drift 40; (2) closer side shot at contact: side 330, up 110, behind-defender bias −120 along axis, FOV 38, handheld micro-shake; (3) tracking shot during the slide following the defender: side 380, up 90, FOV 44. Camera cuts/blends between shots with `EaseInOut` and returns to the player at the end via `SetViewTargetWithBlend(PlayerPawn, TotalOutBlend, VTBlend_EaseInOut)`.
- VFX/SFX slots (all `TSoftObjectPtr<UParticleSystem>` / `TSoftObjectPtr<USoundBase>` plus attach bone names): `ContactSparks` default `/Game/ParagonGreystone/FX/Particles/Greystone/Abilities/Primary/FX/P_Greystone_Primary_Sparks.P_Greystone_Primary_Sparks` spawned at the contact point (weapon blade / between actors, chest height) **and** attached to the defender's visible mesh bone `spine_03` as small body sparks for 0.4 s after contact (the "robot sparks a little" detail; use `SpawnEmitterAttached`), `SlideSparks` (same asset) attached at the defender's feet bone `foot_l`/`foot_r` and re-spawned every 0.12 s while sliding + a dust puff `/Game/ParagonGreystone/FX/Particles/Greystone/Abilities/Primary/FX/P_Greystone_Primary_Impact.P_Greystone_Primary_Impact` at the stop point, `StabImpact` (dust + 1 strong shake at the end of the slide where the sword tip is: use the defender's weapon actor tip socket `TraceEnd` if it has one, else 90 cm in front of the defender at ground level), sounds `ContactSound`, `SlideLoopSound`, `StabSound` (default empty).
- Gameplay result: `DamageToDefender=12` (chip damage even when blocking, bypass block reduction but respect armour), `PoiseDamageToDefender=35`, `bKillDefenderAllowed=false` (leave the defender at ≥1 HP: apply through the project's damage GE `UAAAGE_Damage` with SetByCaller tags like the weapon does, then clamp), `TimeDilation { float AtContact=0.25; float Seconds=0.16 }` (global slow motion pulse around contact using `UGameplayStatics::SetGlobalTimeDilation`, restored by a timer that uses real time — compute the timer duration as `Seconds * Dilation` — and always restored in `EndPlay`/abort).
- **Facing & positions**: before the shot the attacker/defender axis is computed once; the defender is rotated to face the attacker (smoothly over 0.15 s) so the block pose looks right; the slide moves the defender **directly away from the attacker** along the axis projected on the ground; stop short if a sweep (`SweepSingleByChannel`, `ECC_Pawn` ignoring both actors) hits a wall.

## Deliverable B — `URRCinematicDirector` (`UTickableWorldSubsystem`, new `RRCinematicDirector.h/.cpp`)
- `bool PlaySequence(URRCinematicSequence* Seq, AActor* Attacker, AActor* Defender, float SecondsToContact)` (BlueprintCallable) — returns false if one is already playing or either actor is dead; `bool IsPlaying() const`; `void Abort()`; delegates `OnCinematicStarted/Finished` (dynamic multicast).
- `UTickableWorldSubsystem::Tick` drives a small state machine: `Idle → LeadIn → Contact → Slide → Hold → BlendOut → Idle`. Use real/unscaled time for the camera (global time dilation must not slow the camera): accumulate `FApp::GetDeltaTime()`.
- Player control during the scene: for a player-controlled **defender** call `PC->SetCinematicMode(true, /*bHidePlayer*/false, /*bAffectsHUD*/false, /*bAffectsMovement*/true, /*bAffectsTurning*/true)` and set it back at the end (always, also on abort); **the player must not be able to be hit again** during the scene (add the existing invulnerable tag `State.Combat.Invulnerable` through `UAAACombatComponent::ApplyTimedState` for the whole duration + 0.3 s) and the boss attack that started it must not apply its own damage again (P11's boss code applies damage at contact; you add `bool bCinematicHandlesDamage` on `FRRBossAttack`/boss so that when the director accepts the sequence the boss skips its normal damage; set that from the director through a small public method on `ARRBoss`: `NotifyCinematicHandlesContact()`).
- **Defender slide**: the player is a **Mover** pawn (`APawn`, `CharacterMoverComponent`, no CharacterMovementComponent) and the enemy is an `ACharacter`; implement the slide for both: for Mover pawns look at `UAAAMoverUtils` (`AAAAbility/Public/AAAMoverUtils.h`, `ApplyVelocityBurst`, `FApplyVelocityEffect`) and Mover's `QueueInstantMovementEffect`/`ApplyTeleport` docs under `A:/UNREAL/UE_5.8/Engine/Plugins/Experimental/Mover`; the preferred approach is feeding a per-frame velocity effect that matches the derivative of the ease curve so the Mover keeps colliding and keeps ground contact (explain in the report why you chose it). For `ACharacter` use `LaunchCharacter` per frame or `SetActorLocation(sweep)`. The robot must remain grounded and facing the attacker (rotation locked for the duration).
- **Camera**: spawn one transient `ACameraActor` (`SpawnActor` with `ObjectFlags = RF_Transient`), set `bConstrainAspectRatio=false`, update its location/rotation/FOV each tick according to the active shot (compute from the *current* actor positions each frame so the framing follows the action), `PC->SetViewTargetWithBlend(Cam, BlendIn, VTBlend_EaseInOut, 0, true)`; at the end blend back to the previously active view target and destroy the camera after the blend. Add subtle handheld shake to the closer shot (sum of two sines on location, ±1.5 cm, and roll ±0.4°). The game uses a Gameplay Cameras component on the player pawn; verify `SetViewTargetWithBlend` away from and back to the pawn restores it (read `UGameplayCamerasSubsystem`/`UGameplayCameraComponent` usage in the repo; if the component needs re-activation, do it).
- **HUD**: during the scene draw letterbox bars: add to `RRHUD` (new file `RRHUD_Cine.cpp`, method `DrawCinematicBars()` called last in `DrawHUD` for Playing) two black bars top and bottom animating in/out (height 11 % of the screen, 0.3 s ease) while `URRCinematicDirector::IsPlaying()`, and hide the boss HUD / damage FX while playing (fade alpha to 0.2). Use the existing helper `Box()`.
- Hook-up: in `BeginPlay`/`Initialize` bind `FRRBossEvents::OnSpecialAttackContact` (P11) **and add a new event `FRRBossEvents::OnSpecialAttackBegin(ARRBoss*, AActor* Target, float SecondsToContact)`** broadcast by `ARRBoss` when a `bCinematic` attack starts (edit `RRBoss.cpp` accordingly: broadcast right after the attack's montage starts, with `SecondsToContact = WindupSeconds / PlayRate`). On `Begin` the director picks `URRSettings::SpecialSequence` (new `TSoftObjectPtr<URRCinematicSequence>`, default empty ⇒ `NewObject<URRCinematicSequence>` with C++ defaults) and starts the scene **immediately** so the camera is already on its way during the boss's wind-up; on `Contact` it advances to the contact state (if the scene already started it must stay in sync: contact time = `max(SecondsToContact, 0)` measured from `Begin`). If the player evaded (distance to the boss at contact > attack range + 60 and not blocking) the scene aborts gracefully with a blend back (so the cinematic does not play on a clean dodge: decide at `Contact`; before that the camera only moves slightly: shot 1 is wide).
- Always restore global time dilation, cinematic mode, view target and invulnerability on `Deinitialize`, `Abort` and when the world ends.
