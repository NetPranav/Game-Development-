## Deliverable C — boss HUD (canvas, `RRHUD_Boss.cpp`)
New file, method `ARRHUD::DrawBossHud()` (declare in `RRHUD.h`, call from `DrawHUD` after `DrawMatchHUD`, only when Playing, not inventory, not defeat). Use the existing helpers (`Txt`, `Box`, `Frame`, `HGrad`, `Tri`, `Line`, `U()`, `Now`, `CanvasW/H`). Layout like Sekiro: bottom-centre, 52 % screen width: boss name in small tracked caps above; **two health segments** drawn side by side as two rounded-looking bars (the current segment drains with a trailing "ghost" in white then fades, the empty segment shows dark outline only); under them the **posture bar** growing from the centre outward in orange→red, flashing white when broken, with the word "BREAK" while staggered; when `DeathblowReady`/Staggered show a pulsing **"DEATHBLOW"** prompt (red) near the screen centre-bottom. **Perilous glyph**: on `OnPerilousWarning` draw a large red `危`-style mark — Canvas fonts cannot render CJK, so draw a stylised glyph from `RotRect`/`Line`s (a box with a slashing cross and two ticks) projected above the boss head (`Project()` as in the enemy-bar code) and pulsing for the warning time, plus a thin red flash at the screen edges. Phase change: the segment bar plays a short horizontal shatter animation when a segment is removed (the removed segment splits into 6 falling rectangles for 0.6 s) and the text "PHASE II" fades at screen centre for 1.5 s. Also **hide the old per-enemy health bars for the boss** (it has its own HUD) and add the text "THE GREYSTONE FALLS" style victory banner restyle is NOT required.
